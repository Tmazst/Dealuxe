Algorithm:
It will display what user are looking for to buy, collaborate or what they are selling.
This marketing section will both be passive and active. for the passive function the app will display what the users are looking/selling passively to their 
player patners/opponents. Whilst the active the system will use an algorithm to match the users eg. if a user is looking for property to buy and the other 
user is selling property, those users will be automatically macthed with each other in that bracket's first round.
The active config will have 2 levels match matching and Tournament bracket matching. Match matching has a lower propability compared to bracket matching.
Match matching will match the user withing the bracket, which means the user might be guesing if their customer(s) are inside that bracket they are joining, 
sometimes they find that it does(the bracket) have the customers they are lookng for and sometimes not. But the advantage is that the passive will always be there(marketing) as a backup.
In the bracket matching the users who want join open brackets will have privilege to identify brackets that have players who are looking/selling what they need and they can join
on that specific bracket.
In love matching feature, a female with younger age is matched with older male. married users not allowed. 
Please the matchings take place in only in the first round of each tournament, because the other coming rounds are ditermined by winners and losers.
