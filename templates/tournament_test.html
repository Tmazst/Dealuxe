🧪 Tournament Test Arena

Create a local 4-player standard tournament with one manual player + three bots (E10 entry, E40 prize pool). The bot-vs-bot semi-final is auto-settled so the manual player immediately has a real scheduled match against a bot. This mirrors test_tournament_match_flow.py but is driven from the browser instead of the CLI.

How to run the test

  1. Create the tournament with the button above (or press it again to reset with a fresh one).
  2. Log in at /login with the manual player credentials shown below.
  3. Open the bracket, click "Start match" on your scheduled match and play — the bot plays its turns automatically.
  4. Win to advance to the Final; lose to go to the 3rd-Place playoff.
  5. After the Final + 3rd-Place matches, verify the tournament is completed and the prizes are awarded (1st E20 / 2nd E5 / 3rd E2.40).

Roll game test: start a match but don't join it, then press "Roll game" on the bracket. After the 10-minute countdown the waiting player wins by no-show.
